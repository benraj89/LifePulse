POCKDO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

POCKDO (Android application ID com.pockdo.daily) is provided by Ben Raj. For privacy questions or requests, contact benraj8887@gmail.com.

Your records

POCKDO helps you track habits, reminders and personal finances. Habit names, schedules, completion history, financial account labels, balances, amounts, categories, dates, notes, currency settings and any person names you enter are stored in the app's private storage on your device. POCKDO does not require a sign-in account or connect to your bank. A financial account you create in the app is a local record.

We do not operate a server that receives your habit or financial records. We do not intentionally attach those records to crash reports. Error messages can contain contextual information, so avoid entering sensitive information that is unnecessary for your records. POCKDO does not include advertising, Google Analytics or Firebase Cloud Messaging, and we do not sell your personal data.

Optional crash reporting

Crash-report uploading is off by default. You can enable Share crash reports in Privacy & support without changing the app's main features. If enabled, saved and future diagnostics are sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and improve reliability. These can include error stack traces, application state, app and operating-system versions, device model, installation identifiers and app-quality session information, such as session start times and network connection type. Crashlytics includes the Firebase Installations and Sessions libraries.

You can turn sharing off in the same screen. Turning it off takes full effect when the app process next starts. Crash diagnostics may be saved locally while uploading is off; pending reports are discarded on launches with sharing off. Turning sharing off or uninstalling does not erase reports already sent.

Google processes diagnostics as our service provider, including through its subprocessors. Diagnostic uploads use HTTPS. See Firebase's privacy and security information for processing locations, safeguards and retention. Firebase states that crash traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal from live and backup systems begins; this is not a promise that deletion finishes on day 90.

Backups and files you choose to share

Android automatic backup and device transfer are enabled. Depending on your device, Android settings and backup provider, app records and settings may be backed up to your backup account or transferred to another device. Manage these copies through your device and provider settings; we do not operate that backup service.

When you export a finance backup or CSV, POCKDO writes financial information to the location or app you choose. That destination may be a cloud provider. These exports are readable files, without password protection provided by POCKDO. Keep them private and share only with recipients you trust. Deleting app data or uninstalling does not delete exported files or provider backups. Delete those separately. Manual finance exports do not include habit records.

Permissions and other services

Notification permission lets POCKDO show habit reminders. On supported Android versions, optional Alarms & reminders access allows more precise scheduling; reminders may be delayed without that access or because of device restrictions. You can manage these permissions in Android settings. POCKDO does not request access to your address book or device location.

The app uses Google Play to check for and install available app updates. Google Play and any external email, browser, file or backup service you choose also process information under their own privacy terms. Visiting this policy on a hosted website may expose standard request information, such as your IP address, to that hosting provider. The policy bundled inside POCKDO can be read offline.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until you delete them in the app, clear POCKDO's storage through Android settings, or uninstall it. Clearing storage removes the local database and settings; Android may offer to restore a backup on a later installation. Review your backup settings if you do not want a copy restored.

For questions about data already sent to Firebase, or for access or deletion requests, email benraj8887@gmail.com. We will assess what can be located and removed and respond subject to applicable requirements. Reports use installation identifiers rather than a POCKDO sign-in account, so your email address alone may not identify a report. We cannot remotely erase records stored only on your device or copies held by a provider you chose.

If you contact us, your email address and message are used to respond and handle your request and are kept only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Do not send private finance backups unless they are necessary and you choose to share them.

Security

Local data uses Android's app-private storage protection. POCKDO does not add a separate password or encryption layer to its local database or exported files. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep the operating system up to date. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and policy changes

POCKDO is a general-purpose productivity app, not a service designed for children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated policy will show a revised date. Material changes will be explained in the app when appropriate. Contact: Ben Raj, benraj8887@gmail.com.
